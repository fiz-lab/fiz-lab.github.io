AI for Network

We bring AI into the radio access network, from deep learning to multimodal large language models. Models read channels, traffic and context, then schedule, allocate and orchestrate wireless resources on their own.

Network for Physical AI

Robots driven by vision-language-action models must upload what they see and get their next actions back within tight deadlines. We co-design uplink scheduling, edge GPU sharing and network orchestration over AI-RAN so robot fleets act in real time.

AI for Sensing

Wireless signals can see. We use deep learning to turn radio echoes into detection, localization and tracking, and integrate sensing with communication (ISAC) so the network perceives its surroundings while it serves them.
